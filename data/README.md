Property Information

We are pleased to offer you online access to the Assessor-Treasurer's Office property information. While we make every effort to produce and publish the most current and accurate information possible, portions of this data may not be current or correct. Neither Pierce County nor the Assessor-Treasurer's Office makes any warranty, express or implied, with regard to the accuracy, reliability or timeliness of this data, and shall not be held liable for losses caused by its use. Data is provided for informational purposes only, and may be incomplete for determination of value. 

Data downloads contain zipped, pipe ("|") delimited text files and are updated weekly.


For additional information regarding Pierce County Assessor-Treasurer data, please contact us at PCATR@piercecountywa.gov.
